<Title>Noma UI usage rules</Title>

<Description>
Noma components present state supplied by an owning workflow. They do not infer payment, delivery, custody, authorization, approval, or business completion.
</Description>

## Required practice

- Use semantic and component tokens rather than raw colour values.
- Supply readable labels, exact material timestamps, and canonical minor-unit money strings.
- Keep pending, failed, uncertain, superseded, and final states distinct.
- Keep evidence metadata safe and place authorization in the owning application workflow.

## Prohibited practice

- Do not import database, provider, or secret-bearing data into stories.
- Do not use a visual story as an authorization bypass or a production route.
- Do not update screenshot baselines in CI or accept a change without human review.
